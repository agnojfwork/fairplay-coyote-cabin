Discover Adventure & Comfort at Coyote Cabin

The cabin sits on 6 acres so you will enjoy plenty of privacy (and will most likely see wildlife). However, there is a horse barn on the adjacent property that the owners may use at times. The house and the barn share the long driveway as well. The barn is not part of the 6 acres our guests enjoy and we will not disturb any of our guests (however feel free to wave if you see us!). The house and the barn are separated by a large aspen grove and no horses live on site at this time.

Coyote Cabin is 25 miles to Breckenridge and 5 miles from the town of Fairplay, 45 mins to Buena Vista for whitewater rafting, or 1 hour from Salida. It's 1.5 hrs from Downtown Denver and 2 Hours to Denver International Airport. It is located within walking distance to Pike-San Isabel National Forest for easy hiking, back country skiing, snow shoeing, bike riding, etc. While our roads are maintained by Park County, it is best to have a 4WD or AWD in winter months (not necessary in Summer months). The last 1 mile to the property is unpaved.
